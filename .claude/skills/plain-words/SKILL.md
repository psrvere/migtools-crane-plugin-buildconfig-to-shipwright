---
name: plain-words
description: Write documents, pages and notes that are easy to read and understand. Use whenever writing or rewriting text other people will read.
---

Write like one person telling a colleague what they found.

Plain words. If the reader would have to look a term up, say what it does. Names of fields, repositories and commands stay, named once.

Clear. The finding first, then the evidence, then what it changes. A reader with no other context can follow.

Short. One idea per sentence, about twenty words. One point per paragraph, six lines at most, or split it.

Concrete. A number, a name or a mechanism, never a feeling.

Keep what is true. Numbers, names, refs, ids, links and markup stay exactly as they are. Only the words around them change.

No AI tells. No em dashes, no colons joining clauses, no bold labels that restate the line, no groups of three for their own sake, no puffery, sentence-case headings, no emoji. The full list is the unslop skill; read it once. If `/unslop` is not installed, apply the rules above as written.
